Control & governance

Rules, Data Ownership, and Publishing

The page keeps calculations out of the input grid so routine updates cannot overwrite them.

01

Protected calculation

Projected and Forecast Dates

  1. Projected certification = hire or transfer date + 35 calendar days.
  2. Certification forecast = assigned OJT date + 21 calendar days.
  3. If no OJT date is assigned, forecast from the best-guess SIDA date + 21 calendar days.
  4. If neither operational date is available, use the 35-day projected date as the baseline forecast.

Actual certification remains a separate input so variance to the projected date can be measured.

02

Protected calculation

Supporting Dates

Probation end
Hire or transfer date + 60 calendar days
Safety class end
Hire or transfer date + 3 calendar days
Days in training
Current date minus hire or transfer date
Days +/- projected
Projected certification date minus actual certification date
03

Readiness logic

Attention States

  • Delayed — projection is past due.
  • Blocked — a prerequisite has an issue or a weekly milestone is failed or behind.
  • At risk — progress trails the schedule or projected certification is approaching.
  • On track — no current exception.
